            Copying between the chains

              Copy a channel's funding transaction

              This is the one setting here that puts money at risk rather than protecting it. Copying a channel's funding transaction to the other chain creates money there that is not there now — and once it is there, that channel can be closed against you on both chains instead of one.

              The ordinary advice during a split is not to open channels, rather than to make the ones you have span both chains. Leave this off unless you know why you want it.
